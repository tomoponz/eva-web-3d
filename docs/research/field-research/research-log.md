# Field Research Log

## 2026-09-06 — Rebuild EVA Cage macro spatial organization

Investigated:
The large-scale relationship between EVA storage/maintenance bays, restraint release, linear-rail transfer, and the launch/injection facility in Rebuild-era official and official-adjacent material.

Key findings:
The cage is best modeled as a launch-staging node rather than a sealed static garage. Official EVA information describes restraint-panel removal, transfer by linear rail to an injection lane, and launch; official licensed display material presents three adjacent EVA bays and a launch facility described as over 100 m tall.

## 2026-09-06 — Rebuild EVA Cage restraint structures

Investigated:
The distinction between removable cage restraint panels and the restraints integrated into the dedicated mobile transport/injection platform.

Key findings:
Official Evangelion information places restraint-panel removal before linear-rail transfer. BANDAI SPIRITS' licensed `:序` transport platform is explicitly a restraint device during rapid transport to the surface, and the later dedicated restraint/mobile-launcher product identifies safety-device release points at both shoulders and both arms after injection. The sources do not establish whether cage panels and launcher restraints are wholly separate hardware families.

Sources:
- EVA-INFO, 2020-06-11, SMALL WORLDS Evangelion Hangar introduction.
- SMALL WORLDS, Evangelion Hangar area page.

## 2026-09-06 — Rebuild EVA Cage catwalk layout

Investigated:
Minimum defensible service-deck topology around a Rebuild-era EVA cage, using official SMALL WORLDS imagery and the Khara-cooperated Fuji-Q Seventh Cage reconstruction.

Key findings:
A broad human-scale floor apron is strongly supported. SMALL WORLDS official imagery confirms at least one raised wall-mounted service balcony with guardrail and stair access, while Fuji-Q material supports lateral guarded circulation beside Unit-01. No traceable official source was found for the Gemini Foundation's `5–8 catwalk levels`, so exact level count remains unknown.

Sources:
- SMALL WORLDS, Evangelion Hangar area and official hangar images.
- EVA-INFO, 2020-06-11 and 2023-07-20 SMALL WORLDS coverage.
- Fuji-Q / Fuji-Q Highland, 2010 Seventh Cage press release produced with Studio Khara cooperation.
- EVANGELION.JP, 2010 full-scale Seventh Cage announcement.

## Image-search note

Web image search was used only to locate contextual visual references. No anime screenshot, scan, setting sheet or copyrighted frame was downloaded into the repository.

Two recurring visual references were useful:
- Episode 22: enclosed pale green passenger cabin with a horizontal colored band.
- Episode 12: moving lift shot with strong metal-grid / grille visual language.

The images are treated as visual observations, not as authorization to archive or reproduce copyrighted frames.

## Evidence conclusion

High-confidence:
- TV NERV has an enclosed multi-person passenger elevator.
- Episode 22 uses it for actual upward level-to-level circulation.
- at least two people are carried in Episode 22; three pilots ride an elevator in Episode 12.
- therefore the Foundation's non-enclosed-cage description cannot be a universal rule for all NERV personnel lifts.

Medium-confidence:
- Episode 12 represents a visibly more industrial pilot-transport lift variant.
- multiple lift visual classes should be represented in Web 3D.

Unresolved:
- exact cabin dimensions;
- exact shaft/counterweight/rope geometry;
- exact door motion;
- floor IDs and stop graph;
- whether Episode 12's grid is cabin or shaft structure;
- geometry differences between Episode 22 OA and video-format revisions.

## Visual-search note

Web image search was used to look for a front-on TV-series Heaven's Door frame. It returned Terminal Dogma contextual imagery but not a sufficiently attributable/high-quality gate view for geometry extraction. No copyrighted frame was downloaded or stored.

## Evidence conclusion

High-confidence operational claims:
- Heaven's Door is a real named door/boundary in Terminal Dogma.
- Lilith is beyond it.
- a final safety device is released immediately before it opens in Episode 24.
- the physical gate should be kept conceptually separate from the A.T.-Field containment barrier.

Unresolved:
- actual leaf geometry and kinematics;
- exact aperture dimensions;
- actuator and local access hardware;
- EVA-scale normal clearance;
- physical relationship between the final safety device and the gate mechanism.

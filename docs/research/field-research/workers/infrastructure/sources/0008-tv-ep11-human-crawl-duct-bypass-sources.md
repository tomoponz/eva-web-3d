## Image-search note

Current image searches were performed for Episode 11 human duct/crawl imagery. Results reliably surfaced the R-07 door, generic corridor imagery, and unrelated Episode 11 stills, but did not yield a clean, well-attributed frame of the exact human duct interior suitable for strong geometry observations.

No anime screenshot or copyrighted production frame was downloaded or stored in the workspace.

Therefore this cycle intentionally leaves duct section, materials, paneling and hatch details unresolved.

## Evidence conclusion

Supported at high confidence from scene/dialogue evidence:
- the pilots crawl through a duct as a shortcut;
- a later blocked door causes a deliberate duct-bypass decision;
- the later route again requires crawling;
- the duct is human-passable but low-clearance;
- the later duct reconnects to the EVA-area boundary.

Supported only as secondary terminology:
- the human passage is specifically an `air-conditioning duct`.

Unresolved:
- exact duct profile and dimensions;
- whether both crawl events use one continuous duct network;
- exact breach surface/opening method;
- permanent maintenance-hatch geometry;
- exact outlet configuration at the EVA-area boundary;
- HVAC equipment, dampers, lights, and materials.

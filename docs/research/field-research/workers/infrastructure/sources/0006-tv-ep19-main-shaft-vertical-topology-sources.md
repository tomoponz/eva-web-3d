## Image-search note

Image search was used as a locator for Episode 19 visual references, but the returned public images mostly emphasized Zeruel/Unit-01 combat rather than a clean Main Shaft section. No copyrighted frame was downloaded or stored in the research workspace.

The strongest shaft-specific visual evidence in this cycle therefore remains the frame-by-frame cut description in S6, not image-search thumbnails.

## Evidence conclusion

High-confidence:
- Main Shaft exists as a named TV infrastructure element in Episode 19.
- final defensive armor loss exposes it;
- Zeruel can enter and descend through it;
- it leads toward Central Dogma;
- its breach triggers total personnel evacuation;
- its clear space is at least Angel/EVA-scale.

Medium-confidence:
- the shaft is visually presented as a large open vertical void seen from below;
- at least one large pipe/pipe-like utility element is visible in the descent shot;
- the shaft has a direct or near-direct lower interface with occupied command/bridge space.

Unresolved:
- plan cross-section;
- diameter/span/depth;
- wall construction;
- maintenance access;
- guide/hoist machinery;
- normal operating function;
- exact Cage/catapult relationship.

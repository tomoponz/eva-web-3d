## 2026-09-06 — Rebuild EVA Cage placement / posture

Investigated:
Visible foot contact, rear restraint / cradle, neutral stored posture, and whether current evidence supports a rear-only suspension model.

Key findings:
Official SMALL WORLDS full-body imagery shows all three EVAs upright with both feet visibly contacting lower support pads while substantial rear restraint / cradle structures rise behind the bodies. The safe implementation is therefore a combined lower-support + rear-restraint envelope with unknown load distribution. No traceable official source found in this cycle establishes rear-only suspension with unsupported feet. The stored pose reads upright but passive / slightly forward-contained, with arms hanging down.

## 2026-09-06 — Rebuild EVA Cage lower pit / liquid

Investigated:
Whether anything below EVA foot/lower-body level is demonstrably a liquid basin or deep pit, whether the liquid can be identified as LCL/cooling water, and whether a flooded state is general to Rebuild Cage geometry.

Key findings:
Fuji-Q's Studio-Khara-cooperated Seventh Cage reconstruction officially anchors a flooded-looking lower-body composition to Shinji's first encounter scene; two independent contemporary reports identify the attraction liquid as LCL and one confirms real liquid was used. However, the Fuji-Q official release itself does not name the fluid or define pit depth. SMALL WORLDS' official Rebuild Hangar instead shows full-body EVAs on foot bases over a dry operational apron. The correct production stance is therefore configurable dry/flooded lower-zone states, with deep pit, cooling-water function, and canonical film-fluid identity remaining UNKNOWN.

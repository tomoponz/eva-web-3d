# Rebuild EVA Cage lower pit / liquid — visible basin evidence, dry-floor counterevidence, and identification uncertainty

## Scope

This unit asks one narrow question: what is actually supported below EVA foot / lower-body level in Rebuild-era Cage representations, and can any visible liquid be identified as LCL, cooling water, or another fluid?

The unit deliberately separates:

1. visible liquid-like surface;
2. whether the exhibit uses real liquid;
3. whether that liquid is identified as LCL;
4. whether a deep pit exists below the EVA;
5. whether liquid is a permanent / general Cage feature rather than a scene-specific state.

It does not research the wider NERV drainage, coolant, or LCL plant infrastructure.

## Continuity

- Primary target: Rebuild (`:序`-anchored Seventh Cage / early Rebuild visual language)
- Key reconstruction A: Fuji-Q `EVANGELION:WORLD — 実物大初号機建造計画` (2010), officially described as recreating Shinji's first encounter with EVA-01 in the Seventh Cage, produced with Studio Khara's full cooperation.
- Key reconstruction B: SMALL WORLDS Evangelion Hangar, explicitly presented as the Cage from Rebuild of Evangelion.
- TV / EoE are not merged into this finding.

## Summary

There is credible evidence for a **liquid basin / flooded lower zone in at least one officially sanctioned Seventh Cage reconstruction**, but current evidence does not justify turning that into a universal Rebuild Cage rule.

The 2010 Fuji-Q official press release states that its life-size EVA-01 bust recreates the Seventh Cage scene in which Shinji first meets the EVA, and that the attraction was developed with Studio Khara's full cooperation. The press-release hero image shows the lower visible portion of the EVA disappearing into a dark red, reflective basin-like surface. The press release itself does **not** name the fluid.

Two independent contemporary on-site reports, GIGAZINE and Mynavi News, explicitly describe the lower body as submerged in `LCL` / `LCL溶液`; GIGAZINE additionally states that real liquid was used in the installation. This is useful evidence for **how the sanctioned attraction represented the scene**, but it remains secondary evidence for the fluid's canonical identity in the film.

By contrast, SMALL WORLDS' official Rebuild Hangar image shows Units 02, 00 and 01 standing full-body on visible foot-support bases above a broad dry-looking apron. No exposed liquid pool is visible in front of or beneath the feet in that official view.

Therefore:

- `liquid exists in some sanctioned Seventh Cage representation` = supported;
- `the attraction liquid was presented / understood as LCL` = moderately supported by independent contemporary secondary reports;
- `all Rebuild Cage bays contain a deep LCL/cooling-water pit` = unsupported;
- exact pit depth, hydraulic function, drainage, cooling role, and film-canon fluid identity = `UNKNOWN`.

For Web 3D, the lower Cage must remain configurable rather than hard-coded as a bottomless flooded pit.

## Findings

### Finding 1 — Fuji-Q officially anchors its life-size EVA-01 scene to the Seventh Cage

**Claim:** Fuji-Q's 2010 `EVANGELION:WORLD` life-size EVA-01 bust is officially described as a recreation of the Seventh Cage scene where Shinji first encounters EVA-01.

**Evidence class:** `OFFICIAL_META`

**Confidence:** High for attraction intent; Medium for using every attraction detail as exact film geometry.

**Evidence:** Fuji-Q's official press release states that the life-size EVA-01 bust recreates the `第7ケージ（格納庫）` scene and that the project was created under Studio Khara's full cooperation.

**Sources:**

- Fuji Kyuko / Fuji-Q official press release (2010-07-26): https://www.fujikyu.co.jp/data/news_pdf/20100728103552_HLevangelion20100726.pdf
- Evangelion official Rebuild blog announcement: https://www.evangelion.jp/2_0/blog/archive272.html

**Assessment:** The attraction is stronger than an ordinary fan reconstruction, but it is still an attraction and should not automatically be promoted to exact production layout.

### Finding 2 — The official Fuji-Q press image visibly presents a red liquid-like lower basin

**Claim:** The official Fuji-Q press-release image shows the visible EVA-01 bust terminating into / above a dark red reflective lower area that reads as a liquid basin rather than a dry foot-level floor.

**Evidence class:** `VISUAL_OBSERVATION`

**Confidence:** Medium-High that a liquid-like basin is part of the attraction image; Low for exact depth or film geometry.

**Evidence:** The hero image on page 1 of Fuji-Q's official press-release PDF shows EVA-01's lower visible body entering a dark red horizontal surface occupying the bottom foreground of the Cage composition. The lower body and feet are not visible.

**Sources:**

- Fuji-Q official press-release PDF, first-page image: https://www.fujikyu.co.jp/data/news_pdf/20100728103552_HLevangelion20100726.pdf

**Assessment:** The image supports a flooded / basin-like presentation. The official text does not identify its substance or depth.

**Web 3D classification:** `Can be texture/material` for a shallow visual surface in a scene variant; `Unknown` for pit depth and collision volume.

### Finding 3 — Two contemporary independent reports identify the attraction fluid as LCL

**Claim:** Contemporary on-site reports independently describe the Fuji-Q lower-body liquid as LCL / LCL solution.

**Evidence class:** `SECONDARY`

**Confidence:** Medium for the attraction's intended label; Low-Medium for canonical film identification.

**Evidence:**

- GIGAZINE states that only the upper chest is visible, the remaining body is in `LCL溶液`, and that real liquid was used.
- Mynavi News states that the body is properly immersed in LCL.

These are separate publications rather than duplicated official copy.

**Sources:**

- GIGAZINE, 2010-07-23: https://gigazine.net/news/20100723_fujiq_eva_evangelion_1st/
- Mynavi News, 2010-07-16: https://news.mynavi.jp/article/20100716-eva/3

**Assessment:** This supports recording `LCL` as the attraction's apparent / reported fluid identity. Because the Fuji-Q official release itself does not name the fluid, this cycle does not promote `Seventh Cage liquid = canonical LCL` to `CANON_EXPLICIT` or `OFFICIAL_META`.

### Finding 4 — SMALL WORLDS provides strong dry-floor counterevidence

**Claim:** The official SMALL WORLDS Rebuild Hangar representation shows full-body EVAs on visible foot bases and a broad dry-looking floor/apron, with no exposed liquid pool visible beneath the feet in the principal official image.

**Evidence class:** `VISUAL_OBSERVATION`

**Confidence:** High for the official exhibit image; Medium-Low for exact film-state geometry.

**Evidence:** Official `03.jpg` shows Units 02, 00 and 01 standing on small rectangular foot-support bases. The foreground is a broad tiled / panelized floor populated by staff figures. No liquid surface is visible around the feet.

**Sources:**

- SMALL WORLDS Hangar page: https://smallworlds.jp/area/eva_hangar/
- SMALL WORLDS official image `03.jpg`: https://smallworlds.jp/wp/wp-content/themes/smallworlds/area/eva_hangar/03.jpg
- EVA-INFO introduction: https://www.eva-info.jp/9321

**Assessment:** A dry operational floor state is supported by a later official Rebuild reconstruction. This prevents generalizing the Fuji-Q flooded presentation to every Cage state or bay.

**Web 3D classification:** `Must be geometry` for the dry floor/apron in the current evidence-backed default blockout.

### Finding 5 — No current source establishes a deep cooling-water / LCL pit

**Claim:** This cycle found no traceable official source defining a deep pit under the EVA, its depth, or its fluid as cooling water or LCL.

**Evidence class:** `UNKNOWN`

**Confidence:** High that the current evidence gap exists; this does not prove no pit exists in film continuity.

**Evidence:**

- Fuji-Q official material establishes a Seventh Cage reconstruction but does not describe pit depth or fluid function.
- Secondary Fuji-Q reports identify a visible attraction liquid as LCL but do not establish film-layout depth.
- SMALL WORLDS official imagery shows a dry floor / base state instead of a visible deep pool.
- No official source found in this cycle uses terms equivalent to `cooling-water pit`, `lower LCL pit`, or gives dimensions / engineering function.

**Candidate conflicting material:** Gemini Foundation describes a deep lower cooling-water / LCL pit. That stronger claim remains untraced to an identifiable official source.

**3D consequence:** Do not model a bottomless or deep flooded shaft below the EVA as fixed structural truth. Preserve a modular lower-zone socket for future audited evidence.

## Visual Observations

### Fuji-Q official press-release image

- EVA-01 is shown only from the upper torso / head upward.
- The bottom of the composition is occupied by a dark red glossy horizontal region.
- The lower body is concealed below this region.
- Side walls and large mechanical elements frame the bust.
- The image alone cannot establish depth; a shallow theatrical basin could produce the same visible result.

### SMALL WORLDS official image `03.jpg`

- The EVAs are full-body and upright.
- Both feet contact visible rectangular bases.
- A broad dry-looking apron extends in front of the bays.
- Staff figures occupy that floor.
- No exposed liquid is visible around the feet.
- This is a different lower-zone presentation from the Fuji-Q Seventh Cage reconstruction.

## Spatial Implications

The current lower-zone blockout should be represented as configurable states rather than one canonical hard-coded structure:

### `DRY_OPERATIONAL_FLOOR`

- broad service apron;
- visible foot-support / base interfaces;
- staff circulation;
- compatible with restraint removal and rail-transfer staging.

### `FLOODED_FIRST_ENCOUNTER_VARIANT`

- red / amber liquid-like surface around the lower EVA volume;
- lower body can be occluded beneath the surface;
- basin depth remains adjustable / placeholder;
- should be tagged as attraction-backed / audit-needed rather than default canon.

Do not infer a shared deep reservoir beneath all three bays without new evidence.

## Human-Scale Implications

A liquid configuration substantially changes circulation:

- personnel cannot use the same immediate floor zone if it is physically flooded;
- edge railings / side access would become important;
- any boat or floating equipment seen in an attraction should not be imported into film geometry without direct evidence;
- the dry SMALL WORLDS state clearly supports staff-at-foot circulation.

Therefore human circulation and liquid state should be linked in the scene-state system instead of simultaneously forcing both incompatible layouts.

## 3D Modeling Implications

### Must be geometry

- Dry default: bay floor / apron and EVA foot-support bases.
- A clearly bounded lower-zone volume capable of accepting an alternate basin surface later.

### Can be procedural

- liquid surface enabled / disabled by scene state;
- basin surface elevation;
- wet-edge / reflection decals;
- fog / vapor density if later evidence supports it.

### Can be texture/material

- dark red / amber reflective liquid-like surface for the Fuji-Q-inspired variant;
- wetness and reflection should not imply LCL canonically in metadata.

### Requires unique modeling

- only if later evidence establishes a specific lower pit wall, drain, pump, gate, or submerged support structure.

### Unknown

- pit depth;
- fluid volume;
- drainage / cooling mechanics;
- submerged support geometry;
- whether the film uses the same liquid state shown by Fuji-Q.

## Lighting / Material Implications

The Fuji-Q reconstruction demonstrates a useful visual effect: a dark red lower surface can act as a strong color reflector under the purple / green EVA body. This is a **visual reference**, not a canonical PBR specification.

For Web 3D:

- keep the liquid material physically plausible but editable;
- avoid fixing a specific refractive index, opacity, viscosity, or color from attraction photography;
- use the dry floor as the evidence-backed default until audit chooses a target shot / state.

## Scale Evidence

### Official

- Fuji-Q press release: life-size EVA-01 bust approximately 9 m high and 16 m wide as an attraction object. These are attraction dimensions, not Cage pit dimensions.

### Relative

- Fuji-Q liquid surface sits below the visible upper-body portion of the bust.
- SMALL WORLDS dry floor sits directly beneath the EVA foot bases.

### Unknown

- liquid depth;
- lower pit depth;
- floor-to-liquid level difference in film continuity;
- whether any flooded state surrounds the EVA to waist, chest, or another anatomical landmark in the original production layout.

## Contradictions

### Claim A

A sanctioned Seventh Cage reconstruction at Fuji-Q presents EVA-01 as partially submerged in a red liquid basin; contemporary reports identify that attraction liquid as LCL.

**Source A:** Fuji-Q official press release + GIGAZINE + Mynavi News.

### Claim B

The official SMALL WORLDS Rebuild Hangar presents full-body EVAs standing on foot bases above a dry operational floor / apron.

**Source B:** SMALL WORLDS official Hangar page and `03.jpg`.

### Assessment

These do not necessarily describe the same operational state, exact bay, or production layout. They may represent:

- different Cage states;
- first-encounter theatrical staging versus normal maintenance / launch staging;
- different officially sanctioned exhibit interpretations;
- compression / redesign for attraction constraints.

The evidence cannot currently decide among these explanations.

### 3D consequence

Support both dry and flooded scene variants at the data / layout level. Do not merge them into a single impossible simultaneous state.

**Needs deeper research:** Yes.

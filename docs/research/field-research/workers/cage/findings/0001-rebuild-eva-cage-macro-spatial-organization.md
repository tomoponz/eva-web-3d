# Rebuild EVA Cage Macro Spatial Organization — Storage Bay → Restraint → Rail → Launch

## Scope

One narrow research unit: the **macro spatial relationship** between an EVA's storage/maintenance cage, restraint release, rail transfer, and the launch/injection system in Rebuild-era material.

This cycle does **not** attempt to settle catwalk count, liquid-pit identity, crane layout, Entry Plug access, detailed piping, or TV/EoE continuity.

## Continuity

Primary target: **Rebuild**, especially `ヱヴァンゲリヲン新劇場版：序` / `:破`-era representations.

TV / EoE: **Unknown in this cycle**. No attempt is made to merge their cage geometry with Rebuild.

## Summary

The strongest current evidence supports treating the Rebuild EVA Cage as a **launch-staging architecture**, not merely a static hangar room. Official Evangelion information describes a sequence in which an EVA is held in a cage, restraint panels are removed, the unit is transferred by **linear rail** to a launch lane, and then injected/launched. A BANDAI SPIRITS product explicitly identifies the `:序` transport platform as a device that restrains the EVA while transporting it rapidly to the surface. Officially promoted SMALL WORLDS material presents Units 01, 00, and 02 in adjacent bays, giving a useful—but exhibition-adapted—reference for a repeated modular bay language.

The “100 meters or more” figure found in official EVA information refers to the **injection site / launch facility**, not securely to the cage chamber itself. It must not be copied into the scene as a cage-height canon value.

## Findings

### Finding 1 — The cage is functionally connected to the launch system

**Claim:**
For a Rebuild-inspired 3D scene, the cage should be represented as one stage in a continuous operational chain: **stored/maintained under restraint → restraint release → rail transfer → launch lane / injection system**.

**Evidence class:** `OFFICIAL_META`

**Confidence:** High for the functional sequence; Medium for exact geometry.

**Evidence:**
EVA-INFO's official 2020 introduction to the SMALL WORLDS Evangelion Hangar describes restraint panels being removed, the three EVAs being moved by linear rail to the launch lanes, and then launched. SMALL WORLDS' current hangar description repeats the same sequence. This is an officially promoted Evangelion exhibition representation, useful as production-adjacent evidence but not a substitute for film-frame geometry.

**Sources:**
- https://www.eva-info.jp/9321
- https://smallworlds.jp/area/eva_hangar/

### Finding 2 — A dedicated transport platform is part of the Rebuild launch interface

**Claim:**
The transition from cage to surface launch should reserve a dedicated **EVA-sized transport/restraint platform** rather than assuming the unit simply walks from the cage into a shaft.

**Evidence class:** `OFFICIAL_META`

**Confidence:** High for `:序` transport-platform function.

**Evidence:**
BANDAI SPIRITS' officially licensed RG EVA-01 DX Transport Platform Set identifies the platform as the one appearing in `ヱヴァンゲリヲン新劇場版：序` and states that it reproduces the scene in which the EVA is restrained while being transported rapidly to the surface.

**Sources:**
- https://www.bandaispirits.co.jp/products/search/detail.php?grp_id=5325&prd_id=4573102590152000
- https://www.evastore.jp/shop/g/gC7004111/

### Finding 3 — Official exhibition material supports a repeated parallel-bay visual language

**Claim:**
A Rebuild-inspired prototype can use **multiple adjacent vertical cage cells** as a plausible macro composition, with a large EVA standing/restrained in each repeated bay. This should remain a modular visual hypothesis rather than a fixed canon floor plan.

**Evidence class:** `VISUAL_OBSERVATION` + `OFFICIAL_META`

**Confidence:** Medium.

**Evidence:**
The officially promoted SMALL WORLDS hangar is described as storing and maintaining Units 01, 00, and 02. Its published hangar photography shows the three units aligned in adjacent tall bays separated by massive wall/panel modules. The exhibition may compress or combine film locations and moments, so exact bay spacing and count are not promoted to canon.

**Sources:**

- https://smallworlds.jp/area/eva_hangar/
- Published hangar photo on SMALL WORLDS: https://smallworlds.jp/wp/wp-content/themes/smallworlds/area/eva_hangar/03.jpg

### Finding 4 — `第7ケイジ` is an official Rebuild-era naming anchor

**Claim:**
The scene in which Shinji first encounters EVA-01 is officially associated with **第7ケイジ / Seventh Cage** in Rebuild-era promotional material.

**Evidence class:** `OFFICIAL_META`

**Confidence:** High for the 2010 official promotion; Unknown for whether the same numbering should be generalized across all continuities or all production documents.

**Evidence:**
EVANGELION.JP's 2010 announcement for `EVANGELION:WORLD -実物大初号機建造計画-` states that the life-size EVA-01 bust was reproduced together with the Seventh Cage, specifically referencing the scene where Shinji first sees EVA-01.

**Sources:**
- https://www.evangelion.jp/2_0/news/det_11125.html
- https://www.evangelion.jp/2_0/blog/archive272.html

### Finding 5 — “100 m+” belongs to the injection facility, not safely to the cage chamber

**Claim:**
The official exhibition text supports an **extreme vertical launch facility**, but does not justify setting the cage room itself to an exact 100+ m height.

**Evidence class:** `OFFICIAL_META`

**Confidence:** High for the wording; High that the conservative interpretation should be used.

**Evidence:**
EVA-INFO and SMALL WORLDS describe the **射出場 / injection site** as being over 100 meters high in the work. The wording does not state that the storage cage chamber itself has that exact height.

**Sources:**
- https://www.eva-info.jp/9321
- https://smallworlds.jp/area/eva_hangar/

## Visual Observations

From the officially published SMALL WORLDS hangar photograph:

- Three EVA units are arranged in a repeated lateral sequence rather than in one shared open floor with no subdivision.
- Each unit is visually framed by tall, thick, rectilinear wall/panel masses. These masses are large enough to read as architecture, not merely thin equipment racks.
- The unit bodies are exposed toward the main viewing/working side, so the cage reads as a **vertical open-front cell** rather than a closed box.
- Small human figures at floor level provide a strong scale cue. Exact figure placement is exhibition-specific.
- A separate published SMALL WORLDS image shows human-scale railing/stair circulation near the large structural wall; detailed circulation belongs to a later research unit.

These are `VISUAL_OBSERVATION` from an official/promoted exhibition, not direct frame measurements from the films.

## Spatial Implications

A first Web 3D blockout should reserve the following macro zones:

1. **Repeated cage-cell strip** — one or more EVA-sized vertical cells, laterally repeatable.
2. **EVA restraint volume** — enough clearance around torso/shoulders for fixed cage restraint hardware; exact restraint geometry deferred.
3. **Service/viewing side** — open enough to expose the EVA and permit human-scale circulation in front or beside it.
4. **Transfer interface** — a clear route from the cage cell to a dedicated transport platform / rail system.
5. **Launch/injection continuation** — a visually and functionally connected vertical system beyond the cage; do not terminate the space as a dead-end garage.

The current evidence does **not** determine whether the rail runs laterally, rearward, or through a specific wall opening at every stage. Keep that interface parametrically editable.

## Human-Scale Implications

Human-scale references are not decorative. The official SMALL WORLDS presentation explicitly emphasizes viewing the Evangelions from NERV staff eye level, and published photos use tiny staff figures against full-height EVA bays.

For blockout:

- include at least one 1.6–1.8 m placeholder human near the bay floor;
- include one human-scale railing/door/stair reference near the cage wall;
- avoid using the EVA alone as the only scale reference.

These human dimensions are **real-world production references**, not Evangelion canon measurements.

## 3D Modeling Implications

### Must be geometry

- Main cage-cell wall masses / separators.
- EVA-sized opening and structural recess.
- Transport-platform envelope and primary rail/guide path.
- Major transition opening toward the launch/injection system.

### Can be instanced

- Repeated bay wall modules after one master bay is established.
- Repeated large service-panel blocks where the exhibition/reference pattern supports repetition.
- Human-scale railing segments and support posts in later passes.

### Can be procedural

- Parametric bay spacing and bay count.
- Rail/guide continuation length.
- Repeated panel grid on large structural walls.

### Can be decal

- Bay identifiers, EVA unit labels, hazard markings, and technical stencils after signage research.

### Can be simplified at distance

- Distant service rooms and the far continuation of the injection shaft.
- Repeated panel subdivisions that do not affect silhouette.

### Requires unique modeling

- EVA-specific fixed restraint interface.
- Dedicated transport platform and its primary restraint silhouette.
- Transition geometry where cage and launch system mechanically connect.

## Lighting / Material Implications

This cycle does not establish a canonical lighting rig. However, the official exhibition imagery consistently uses a dark high-bay environment with strong local illumination on the EVAs and cool blue service-area light. Treat this only as a **visual reference**, not a canonical photometric specification.

For the prototype, prioritize silhouette readability of the tall bay walls and the EVA-to-human scale relationship before adding complex PBR surface variation.

## Scale Evidence

- **Official / official-meta:** injection site described as **over 100 m high** in official EVA information promoting the SMALL WORLDS hangar.
- **Not established:** exact cage chamber height.
- **Relative:** each bay must accommodate a full EVA body plus restraint/transfer clearances.
- **Estimated / production reference only:** 1.6–1.8 m placeholder human for scale checks.

Do not convert the 100+ m injection-site statement into a fixed cage height.

## Contradictions

No direct source contradiction was confirmed in this cycle.

Potential adaptation issue:

**Claim A:** official/promoted SMALL WORLDS material shows three EVAs in adjacent cage bays and a continuous automated launch sequence.

**Source A:** EVA-INFO / SMALL WORLDS.

**Claim B:** film continuity may show the same functions through different shots, geometry, or temporal staging rather than one literal exhibition floor plan.

**Source B:** not independently verified in this cycle.

**Assessment:** Exhibition geometry may be a compressed or curated synthesis.

**3D consequence:** Use the three-bay arrangement as a modular reference, not a locked canonical plan.

**Needs deeper research:** Yes.

## Unknowns

- Exact cage-cell width, depth, and spacing.
- Whether the film's cage and launch lane occupy one continuous chamber or linked adjacent volumes.
- Exact orientation of linear-rail transfer relative to the cage face.
- Whether all three Units 00/01/02 are simultaneously stored in the same spatial row in the relevant film continuity.
- Whether `第7ケイジ` numbering is stable across scripts, setting books, TV, and Rebuild.
- Whether the EVA is structurally suspended from a rear mount, standing on a lower support, or both in different operational states.
- Number and placement of catwalk decks.
- Existence/identity of a lower liquid pit in this continuity.

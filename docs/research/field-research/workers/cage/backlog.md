## Contradiction

- Lower-zone state ambiguity: Fuji-Q Seventh Cage reconstruction presents a flooded lower-body basin reported by contemporary sources as LCL, while SMALL WORLDS official Rebuild Hangar shows full-body EVAs on dry foot bases / apron. These may be different states or exhibit interpretations; do not merge them automatically.
- Restraint handoff ambiguity: cage `拘束パネル` are removed before rail transfer, while the dedicated mobile injection platform restrains the EVA during high-speed transport. Sources do not establish whether these are fully separate hardware systems or connected states.
- Catwalk-count ambiguity: Gemini Foundation states `5–8` levels, while current official reconstruction evidence only establishes a floor apron plus at least one raised deck/stair. This does not disprove additional levels but leaves the numeric claim unsupported.

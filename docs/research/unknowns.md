# Research Unknowns Registry

Status: normalized v0.1

Unknowns are intentional. Do not fill them with plausible design choices and later forget they were invented.

## EVA Cage geometry

- Exact Cage width, depth, and height.
- Exact bay count, spacing, and whether multiple EVAs are simultaneously stored in one row in film continuity.
- Exact wall cross-section: cylindrical vs hexagonal vs rectangular/other.
- Exact catwalk level count and vertical spacing.
- Exact floor-apron depth, service-deck width, railing height, stair geometry, and route clearances as Evangelion-specific dimensions.
- Exact lower-zone/pit depth.
- Whether the Cage chamber itself has a verified 100+ m height. Current evidence only supports that wording for the injection/launch site.

## EVA support / restraint

- Exact static load path between feet/base, rear support, restraint panels, hidden support, and transport platform.
- Whether the EVA is ever supported primarily or exclusively by a rear mount in the Cage.
- Exact restraint-panel contact points.
- Exact restraint actuator type, motion axis, and travel range.
- Whether Cage restraint panels and mobile launcher restraints are separate hardware systems or connected operational states.
- Whether lower-body restraints exist on the mobile launch platform.

## Entry Plug operations

- Exact Entry Plug insertion axis and angle in Rebuild Cage context.
- Exact loading mechanism and its mounting location.
- Exact clearance dimensions.
- Whether the Plug is loaded while the EVA remains in the same Cage restraint state shown in official/promotional reconstructions.
- Where the pilot boards the Entry Plug in Rebuild operational workflow.
- Whether a permanent or retractable personnel bridge reaches the dorsal port.
- Whether upper service access connects directly to existing wall-side circulation.
- Clearance interaction between Plug insertion/removal and Cage restraint-panel motion.

## Lower liquid / pit

- Identity of the liquid shown/presented in some Cage-related reconstructions.
- Whether it is LCL, cooling water, another industrial fluid, or an exhibition-specific presentation.
- Exact level, depth, optical properties, and operational purpose.
- Whether dry and flooded representations indicate different operational states, different spaces, or incompatible exhibit adaptations.

## Launch connection

- Exact orientation of the linear-rail transfer relative to the Cage face.
- Exact physical adjacency between Cage, transport platform, launch lane, and injection shaft.
- Exact vertical/horizontal split of the launch sequence.
- Exact Cage-to-launch boundary geometry.
- Recovery path after battle and whether it literally reverses the launch route.

## Upper structure / utilities

- Verified existence and geometry of a top gantry crane.
- Verified suspended umbilical arm arrangement.
- Large cable routing and backend power infrastructure.
- Pipe-rack routing and function.
- Ventilation/duct geometry.
- Working-light and warning-light fixture placement.
- Entry/exit doors, access hatches, ladders, and alternative vertical circulation.

## Scale

- Universal EVA height: intentionally unresolved; do not seek one value that covers all continuities/cuts.
- Exact Rebuild Cage dimensions relative to EVA height.
- Exact human-scale component dimensions in production geometry.

## GeoFront / NERV topology

- Primary-source confirmation of the widely repeated `13.75 km / 6 km / 0.9 km / 22 layers` values.
- Exact NERV Headquarters internal floor plan.
- Exact architectural adjacency implied by film editing.
- Detailed connection between Cage infrastructure and the broader NERV internal network.

## Visual / material

- Canonical material identity of major Cage surfaces (concrete, steel, galvanized grating, etc.).
- Canonical physical placement/intensity/color temperature of light fixtures.
- Canonical facility-font family beyond verified Evangelion-associated Matisse usage.

## Next-step rule

When a Field Research cycle resolves one of these items, update the claim register and relevant normalized location/visual/technology document. Do not silently delete the unknown; record what evidence resolved it.
